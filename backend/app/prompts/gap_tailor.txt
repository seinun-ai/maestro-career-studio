Custom (extra) sections are user-defined blocks (Publications, Awards, Volunteer Work).
`section_key` resolves against the stored ExtraSection.key, not its title or list index.
An ExtraSection is a whole section:

when a resolution explicitly targets a custom section. Custom-section evidence contributes to the
ATS score in phase 2, so these ops must never be used to smuggle fabricated experience.

Section indices refer to the resume JSON arrays as given (0-based, disabled entries
included in the numbering). "replace_skills_group" must repeat the FULL item list for
that category, with additions appended.
