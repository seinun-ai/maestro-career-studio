Rules:
- "fragment": a bullet reads as a bolted-on fragment or broken sentence.
- "tense": verb tense inconsistent with the entry's other bullets.
- "summary_mismatch": the summary contradicts or ignores what the body now shows.
- "dangling": text references something the document no longer contains.
