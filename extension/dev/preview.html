The two ways in

These take different paths and that is the whole point. Auto-mount is what a page load does: it runs the detection gate and the stored dismissals, so on a hidden origin it does nothing. Mount directly is what the toolbar action and the hotkey do — it bypasses the dismissal check on purpose, because the origin the user hid is exactly the one they press the button on. A widget that came back from "Mount directly" while the origin is still hidden is CORRECT; test the guard with "Auto-mount" or a reload.

Persistence

Dock: drag the bubble, then Reload — it comes back on the same side at the same offset, because what is persisted is a dock side plus an offset and never an x/y. Resize the window shorter before reloading to watch the re-clamp. Origin hide: hide it, then Reload or Auto-mount — it must stay gone; those are the two buttons that prove it. Storage here is sessionStorage, so it survives a reload and not a new tab.

Collision (Task 12)

Scroll the page until the Save-and-continue bar reaches the bubble, or move the bar to force each outcome. Bar on the right → the bubble hops left. Bar across the full width → nowhere to hop, so it collapses and stays. The state readout reports how many submit controls are observed and how many are on screen; a hop needs the second number above zero.

This tab is not being rendered (document.visibilityState is hidden — a background tab, or an automated pane). A tab that is not rendered delivers no IntersectionObserver entries and runs no animation frames, so collision avoidance cannot be tested here at all and the controls above will do nothing. That is the environment, not a defect: bring this tab to the front. The decision itself is pure geometry and is covered by chooseEvasion's node tests in backend/tests/test_extension_widget.py.
